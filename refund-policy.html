EU consumer information

For eligible online purchases from an EU trader, consumers normally have 14 days after receiving goods to notify the trader that they wish to cancel. Goods generally must then be returned within a further 14 days. The trader normally refunds within 14 days of cancellation, but may wait for the returned goods or evidence of return.

The refund normally includes the original standard delivery charge. A consumer may bear return postage where this was disclosed before purchase. Rules and exceptions depend on the transaction; rights for faulty goods are separate. See the European Union's returns and withdrawal guidance and guarantee guidance.

This reference explains general rights; it does not imply that Velora currently operates a sales or returns service. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
