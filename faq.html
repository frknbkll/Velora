Clear answers about what this website currently offers.

What is Velora?

Velora is a fashion collection showcase. Founder stories and fictional team profiles introduce its creative direction; they do not identify a registered legal operator. See Business Details for the operator information.

Can I place an order?

No. The bag lets you review looks and an indicative subtotal. There is no checkout, payment, delivery booking or stock reservation.

Are prices final, and are there fees?

Amounts are showcase estimates in euros. Browsing and accounts are free. No payment is taken. Taxes, delivery costs and final product information must be confirmed before any future sale; see Shopping Information.

Are the images and fabric details verified?

Product images include AI-created colour and pose previews. Names and descriptions present a design concept; composition, fit, origin and performance are unverified unless confirmed product specifications are supplied. Review ratings on the Reviews page come from account holders' submissions; they are not verified purchases.

How do I leave or change a review?

Sign in on the Reviews page, choose one to five stars, write your review, and optionally add a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo. You can publish one review per account and return to update or delete it. The site does not verify a purchase before accepting a review.

Can I post a review anonymously?

Yes. Your current account name is filled in automatically by default. Select “Post anonymously” to display “Anonymous” publicly instead. Your review stays linked to your account so you can manage it. The option does not hide identifying details you include in your text or photo. You can change this choice when you edit your review.

How do the wishlist and bag work?

They work on the current page. If you allow saved-look storage in Cookie preferences, they can also be remembered on this browser. Withdrawing that choice removes the saved entries here. See the Cookie Policy.

Where are delivery and return details?

This site does not take orders. Shipping arrangements and a return address have not been supplied. The Refund Policy explains the current status and links to general consumer rights.

Can children register?

Accounts are offered only to people aged 16 or over. Registration asks for confirmation, without a birth date. There is no verified parental-permission workflow for younger children.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in and use the deletion option in Your account. You will need your current password and, if enabled, an authenticator or recovery code. Deleting your account also removes your review and review photos from the live database. The last administrator must first arrange another authorized administrator. Saved browser data on other devices must be cleared on those devices.

How do I unsubscribe?

There is no active mailing list and this site does not collect newsletter email addresses or send marketing emails. If a mailing service is introduced, it must provide separate sign-up consent and an unsubscribe link in every marketing message. See Email preferences / unsubscribe.

How can I contact the operator?

Check Business Details. Self-service profile changes, sign-in security and account deletion are available in your account.
