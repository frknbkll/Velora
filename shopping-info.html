Shopping
information.

Velora is currently a brand and collection showcase. No checkout, payment or order processing is available.

Prices and fees

Prices and bag subtotals are indicative showcase amounts in euros, rather than a payable order total. No money is collected and there are no account, membership or subscription fees.

Shipping, tax treatment and other purchase charges have not been set. Before purchases can be offered, the seller must publish the full payable price and any delivery costs before the customer commits. A preview subtotal is not a promise that shipping or taxes would be free.

Product information, size and fit

Collection imagery and colour variants include AI-created previews. Names such as “linen” or “satin” describe the visual design concept; fibre composition, measurements, manufacture and product performance have not been verified. Size choices are illustrative. Catalog inventory fields do not reserve stock or prove that garments are available for sale.

Open a piece and choose Size & fit to read any product-specific guide and fitting notes supplied for it. Fabric composition, fabric weight and country of manufacture appear separately under Fabric & product details when supplied. Missing details are identified there.

How to take your measurements

  • Use a flexible tape, keep it level and avoid pulling it tight.
  • Measure your bust at its fullest part, your natural waist, and the fullest part of your hips.
  • For trousers, measure your inside leg from crotch to your preferred hem.
  • Compare with the specific product guide when available. Check whether its chart gives body or garment measurements; size labels differ between products.

There is no verified collection-wide size chart yet. We do not infer measurements or recommend a size from an image.

Delivery and returns

No shipping destinations, delivery promises or return address are configured. Read the Refund Policy for the current showcase status and general EU consumer information.

Reviews and claims

The Reviews page publishes star ratings, comments and optional photos submitted by signed-in account holders. Reviews describe their submitted experiences; purchases are not verified, and a review is not a verified-buyer endorsement. The rating summary uses submitted reviews rather than illustrative scores.

You can publish one review per account and update or delete it after signing in. Your current account name is shown by default, or you can choose “Post anonymously” to hide that name publicly. An anonymous review remains linked to your account. See the Privacy Policy for review and photo handling. No sustainability certifications or guaranteed garment-performance claims are made.

Find account and website help in the FAQ, or check Business Details for the operator information.
