AI editing or saving an online photograph does not establish permission to reproduce or modify that photograph. The EU's [copyright and licensing overview](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/growing/protecting-intellectual-property/copyright/index_en.htm) explains that permissions can cover different uses. Confirm the actual grant rather than assuming a general licence covers advertising, derivatives and portraits.

## How to clear the unresolved assets

For every original, obtain its original source link, author/rightsholder, licence or purchase reference, permitted commercial and modification uses, attribution requirements and any required model/property releases. Keep the evidence privately; publish attribution where the licence requires it. A stock receipt alone may not resolve every depicted-person or property right.

If the original cannot be identified and cleared, replace it with an independently created or properly licensed asset and update references. Do not claim the current images are licensed stock. The current task does not authorize guessing a provider or purchasing an unknown licence, and no such purchase has been made.

The site remains a local showcase. Rights verification is outstanding before commercial publication. Review the inventory when adding or replacing assets; file hashes in the register identify the reviewed bytes, not legal permission.
