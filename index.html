Velora began with four friends swapping inspiration, passing things around the table, and asking a simple question: what if getting dressed felt less like getting it right and more like being yourself?

What followed is a collection shaped by four points of view: graceful modest silhouettes, thoughtful styling, personal storytelling, and a love of enduring design. Each one distinct. All of them, together.

Get to know all four of us ↗

CREATIVE DIRECTOR & MODEST FASHION DESIGNER

Beyza Keskinci

“Modesty, to me, is its own kind of quiet confidence.”

Beyza believes beautiful clothes should give a woman room to feel completely like herself. As Creative Director, she brings that belief to Velora’s modest luxury pieces: graceful draping, considered coverage, and silhouettes that feel refined without ever feeling restrictive. Her eye is drawn to the small things—a sleeve that moves just right, the softness of a fabric, the calm of a well-balanced shape. Every piece she touches is designed to feel elegant, personal, and unmistakably your own.

BRAND DIRECTOR & MARKETING LEAD

Derya Kasal

“A good story should make you feel like you belong.”

Derya knows a brand is more than what you wear—it is the feeling you carry with you. She shapes how Velora speaks, appears, and welcomes people in, from campaign imagery and brand storytelling to the everyday conversations that bring a community closer. Thoughtful, warm, and always tuned in, she turns four friends’ shared vision into a world women can see themselves in. To Derya, the best kind of connection starts with listening.

Explore Derya’s edit ↗

HEAD OF STYLING & COLLECTION COORDINATOR

Luna Moretti

“It’s the little details that make a look feel like you.”

Luna sees an outfit as a conversation between texture, shape, and the woman wearing it. She is the thoughtful eye behind Velora’s styling: pairing fabrics, finding the accessory that makes a look click, and making sure every piece feels at home alongside the next. Her finishing touch is never about adding more for the sake of it; it is about finding exactly what was missing. With Luna, getting dressed becomes the lovely little ritual of putting it all together.

Explore Luna’s edit ↗

FOUNDER & FASHION DESIGNER

Victoria Mendez

“I wanted to make pieces that still feel right years from now.”

Victoria founded Velora around a simple idea: femininity does not need to choose between feeling timeless and feeling entirely modern. She leads the collection’s design and trend direction, bringing a clear point of view to every silhouette, fabric, and finishing detail. Drawn to enduring elegance rather than the next fleeting thing, Victoria builds a wardrobe designed to stay with you and shift naturally through every version of your life. Velora is her original vision—and a more beautiful story because all four friends are writing it together.
