The people behind the pieces

Four women.
Four points of view.

Meet the four perspectives in Velora’s concept story. The founder profiles are illustrative showcase content; Business Details identify the website operator separately.

Meet the wider team ↗

Creative Director & Modest Fashion Designer

Beyza Keskinci

“Modesty, to me, is its own kind of quiet confidence.”

Beyza believes beautiful clothes should give a woman room to feel completely like herself. She brings that belief to Velora’s modest luxury pieces through graceful draping, considered coverage, and silhouettes that feel refined without feeling restrictive. Her eye is drawn to the small things: how a sleeve moves, the softness of a fabric, and the calm of a well-balanced shape.

Brand Director & Marketing Lead

Derya Kasal

“A good story should make you feel like you belong.”

Derya shapes how Velora speaks, appears, and welcomes people in. From campaign imagery and brand storytelling to the conversations that bring a community closer, she turns four friends’ shared vision into a world women can see themselves in. Her work begins with listening and finding the human feeling at the heart of a story.

Head of Styling & Collection Coordinator

Luna Moretti

“It’s the little details that make a look feel like you.”

Luna sees an outfit as a conversation between texture, shape, and the woman wearing it. She pairs fabrics, finds the accessory that makes a look click, and brings the collection into visual harmony. Her finishing touch is about finding exactly what was missing—not adding more for its own sake.

Founder & Fashion Designer

Victoria Mendez

“I wanted to make pieces that still feel right years from now.”

Victoria founded Velora around the idea that femininity can feel both timeless and modern. She leads the collection’s design and trend direction, bringing a clear point of view to every silhouette, fabric, and finishing detail. She is drawn to enduring elegance and wardrobes that move naturally through different versions of life.
