Policy.

Updated 4 November 2026. This website uses essential account and requested support-chat storage, and offers optional storage for saved looks.

Essential cookies and choices

Sign-in cookie — velora_session: set when you sign in or create an account. It contains an opaque session identifier, is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and is used by the account service. Customer sessions expire after 12 hours, or up to 30 days when you choose to remain signed in. Staff sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or eight hours overall. Signing out expires the current session.

Guest support cookie — velora_support: set only when you open support chat as a guest. It contains an opaque identifier for your private guest conversation, is HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, is limited to the support API path, and expires after seven days. It is used to provide the chat you request, rather than to track browsing. Signed-in support uses your account session. Opening a page without opening chat does not create a guest support session.

Support panel — velora-support-panel: after you first open chat, session storage remembers only whether its panel is open or closed in this tab. This lets the conversation reconnect as you browse this website. It does not contain messages, names or session credentials and ends when the tab's session storage is cleared.

Privacy choice — veloraPrivacyChoice: browser local storage remembers whether you allowed saved-look storage, together with the choice version and dates. It lasts up to 180 days. It records your choice rather than tracking your browsing.

Optional storage for saved looks

With your permission, local storage remembers your wishlist and preview shopping bag on this browser. No order is submitted. The values describe selected products and quantities, not your account password or payment information. They last for up to 180 days, or until you clear them, remove them individually, withdraw this choice or clear your browser's site data.

Until you allow this storage, the wishlist and bag can be used on the current page without being remembered after reloading. Refusing this choice does not block browsing, accounts or account security. Withdrawing it removes the saved wishlist and bag from this browser.

No analytics or advertising categories

No analytics, advertising pixels, social-media embeds or marketing SDKs are enabled. There is no additional category to accept for these purposes. Fonts and the QR-code library are served locally, so loading a page does not request them from Google Fonts or an external QR service.

Change your preferences

Use the “Cookie preferences” control in the footer to review your choice. You can also clear saved shopping data without deleting your account. Your browser's site-data settings can remove local storage and cookies; removing a sign-in cookie signs you out on that device.

Optional storage starts off. Continuing to browse, scrolling or closing a page is not consent. Giving and withdrawing permission use the same preference control.

The Dutch regulator explains cookie and tracking consent. More about account processing is in our Privacy Policy.
