Policy.

This notice explains the current Velora showcase and account service. Updated 2 November 2026.

Who is responsible?

Velora is the name used by this website. The operator's legal identity, business address and contact information appear on Business Details. Fields marked “Not yet provided” are awaiting confirmation. This notice does not certify that a business has been registered or that the website is ready to take orders.

What we collect and why

Your account: your first name, last name, city, country, email address, password hash, account creation date, and the accepted terms version and terms/age-confirmation times. First name, last name, city and country are requested when you create an account; your name appears in your own storefront header while signed in. These details provide and personalize the account service you request. A password is required to protect access; we do not retain it as readable text.

Security: session identifiers, IP address, a browser/device label and activity times help protect accounts and show signed-in devices. Failed-attempt limits and security logs help detect abuse. We rely on the legitimate interest of operating a secure service for this processing.

Optional two-factor sign-in: if you enable it, we retain the information needed to verify authenticator codes and one-use recovery codes. Your setup key is shown for enrollment; recovery codes are shown when generated. These secrets are not saved in browser storage.

Device preferences: with your separate choice, your wishlist and preview bag can be remembered on this browser. Without that choice, they work for the current page without persistent storage. Your cookie choice itself is saved to remember your decision. See the Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for a date of birth, home address, telephone number, identity document or payment details. Browsing does not require an account. This showcase does not process orders, send newsletters, run advertising or perform behavioural profiling.

Who can access it?

Authorized administrators can access account names, cities, countries, email addresses and join dates, and review security activity. The account database and backups are kept by the site operator. A future hosting provider may handle technical request data; no public hosting provider or international transfer arrangement has been identified for the current local installation.

Fonts, images and the QR-code library are served with this site. The QR code is generated on your device; its setup secret is not sent to an external QR service. No analytics, advertising or social-media SDK is enabled. Following an external link takes you to that provider's website.

How long is it kept?

Account details and acceptance records remain until the account is deleted. Customer sign-in sessions expire after 12 hours, or up to 30 days if “Keep me signed in” is chosen. Administrator sessions expire after 30 minutes without authenticated activity or eight hours overall. Expired records are cleaned during service use.

Security events are pruned to at most 2,000 entries and a 30-day window when security records are updated or the service starts. Failed-attempt records use a 15-minute window. An unconfirmed two-factor setup expires after ten minutes; a sign-in verification challenge expires after five minutes.

Your cookie choice lasts up to 180 days. Remembered wishlist and bag entries last for up to 180 days, or until you clear them, withdraw the storage choice, or remove site data in your browser.

Manual database backups can contain earlier account information. Backup expiry and hosting-log retention have not yet been configured. Removing an account deletes live account records; earlier backup copies require the operator to remove them too.

Your choices and rights

Use Your account to view or correct your profile details, change your password, review devices, and delete your account. Deletion requires your current password and, when enabled, a current authenticator or recovery code. Account deletion ends all its sign-in sessions. The final administrator must arrange another authorized administrator before deleting that account.

Use Cookie preferences to change optional device storage or clear saved shopping data. Account deletion on one device cannot reach browser data on other devices; clear site data on those devices separately.

Depending on the processing, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may withdraw optional consent. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing. The operator must publish a working privacy contact for requests that cannot be completed in your account.

You can also contact the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the relevant supervisory authority where you live. The AP's privacy information explains data-protection rights.

Children and teenagers

Accounts are offered to people aged 16 or over. Registration asks for confirmation rather than a birth date. This site does not provide a parental-verification service, so someone under 16 should not create an account. If a child's information was entered, the account can be deleted through its settings; other requests need the operator's privacy contact once published. This site policy is not a claim that one age rule applies to every use of personal data.

Messages and updates

There is no active mailing list and no email address is collected for one. If subscriptions are introduced, their purpose, provider, consent and unsubscribe method must be presented before sign-up. Changes to this notice will appear here with a new update date.
